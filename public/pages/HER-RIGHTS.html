INTERNATIONAL HUMANITARIAN & REFUGEE LAW

Her Rights: Inalienable Protections in Conflict & Displacement

Under international law, women and girls retain binding, non-negotiable human rights during armed conflict and forced displacement-regardless of borders, legal status, or nationality.

Her 6 Core International Rights

01.

PROTECTION

Right to Safety & Freedom from Gender-Based Violence

States and humanitarian authorities bear an affirmative duty to protect displaced women and girls from sexual violence, exploitation, abuse, and trafficking across camps, transit corridors, and urban host settings.

02.

ASYLUM LAW

Right to Asylum & Non-Refoulement

No woman may be forcibly returned (refouled) to a territory where her life or freedom is threatened. Gender-related persecution-including sexual violence, forced marriage, FGM, or severe systemic oppression-constitutes valid grounds for refugee status.

03.

CIVIL STATUS

Right to Legal Identity & Documentation

Displaced women are entitled to individual refugee registration and civil documentation-including birth, marriage, and divorce certificates-on equal terms with men, without requiring a male relative's permission or sponsorship.

04.

HEALTHCARE

Right to Health & Reproductive Care

Access to the highest attainable standard of physical and mental health is non-derogable in crises. This encompasses prenatal and emergency obstetric care, skilled birth attendance, family planning, menstrual hygiene, and trauma counseling.

05.

LIVELIHOODS

Right to Economic Self-Reliance & Property

Refugee and displaced women hold the right to lawful wage-earning employment, vocational training, financial inclusion, and equal housing, land, and property (HLP) rights-including inheritance and post-conflict restitution.

06.

GOVERNANCE

Right to Voice & Peacebuilding Leadership

Displaced women are not passive recipients of aid; they are rights-holders entitled to equal participation in camp governance committees, humanitarian program design, formal peace negotiations, and transitional justice mechanisms.
