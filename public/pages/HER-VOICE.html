A Childhood and a Future Interrupted.

When war reached her home in Homs, Syria, her family endured days without food, surrounded by destruction and fear. Forced to flee for their safety, they left behind the life they once knew. In Jordan, the struggle did not end with finding refuge. Access to basic necessities became another daily challenge, while her children's education and dreams were put on hold.

Her story reveals a painful truth: escaping war does not mean escaping its consequences. For women and families forced to rebuild their lives, the fight for safety, stability, and a future continues long after they leave their homes.
