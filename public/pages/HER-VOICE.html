A short summary of UNHCR's published account. Not a transcription of the video.

When war forced Nawal to flee Sudan, she left behind the life she once knew in search of safety for herself and her family.

But crossing the border did not mean the end of her struggles.

In Egypt, she faces the difficult reality of starting over, carrying the responsibility of providing for her family while navigating life as a refugee. Her story is a reminder that the consequences of war do not end when people escape the violence. For women like Nawal, survival continues long after displacement. Behind every journey is a woman, a family, and a future worth fighting for.
